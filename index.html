Pick your ride

Controls: arrow keys or WASD, or the on-screen buttons on a phone. The loop has a steep hill, two mud pits, a flooded road and a river ford. Grass and mud slow every car, and low cars (Vios, Civic) struggle in deep water, while the Hilux and Jimny cope better. Two traffic lights on the loop: stop fully at red. Obey the speed limit (80 km/h on the open road, 20 km/h in the school zone), give way to the pedestrian on the crossing and to the vehicle on your right at the intersection after the hill, and pull to the right for the ambulance. There is also a 30 km/h city zone around the first traffic light. Each lap you get a driver score: 85 and up is a Professional driver, 76 to 84 a Non-professional driver, and 75 or below Passenger only.
